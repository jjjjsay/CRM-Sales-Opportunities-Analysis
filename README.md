# CRM Sales Opportunities Analysis

B2B sales pipeline analysis on a year of CRM data (Oct 2016–Dec 2017): 8,800
opportunities across 3 regional offices, 30 agents, 7 products, and 85
accounts. Diagnoses team/agent performance, quarter-over-quarter trends, and
product win rates, and turns the findings into prioritized business
recommendations.

**[Read the full write-up (PDF/DOCX report)](./CRM_Sales_Opportunities_Analysis.docx)**

## Key findings

- **Win rate fell 22 points over three quarters** (82.1% → 60.3%, Q1–Q4 2017)
  while deal volume held flat — a conversion problem, not a pipeline problem.
- **Five agents are converting 6–14 points below the company average**
  despite carrying average-or-above deal volume — a coaching gap.
- **Two products (GTX Pro, GTX Plus Pro) generate 61% of won revenue** at
  above-average win rates; a third high-volume product (MG Advanced) is
  underperforming by ~$110K in recoverable revenue.
- Found and fixed a real data-quality bug: the product "GTX Pro" was
  recorded under two different spellings across 17% of the pipeline,
  silently excluding it from every product-level metric until corrected.

## Data

Source: [Maven Analytics — CRM Sales Opportunities](https://www.mavenanalytics.io/data-playground)
(fictitious B2B computer-hardware company). Not included in this repo —
download it from the link above and place the four CSVs in `data/`.

## Run it

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python crm_sales_analysis.py
```

Prints summary metrics to the console and saves 5 charts to `figs/`.

## Methodology highlights

- **Win rate** = Won ÷ (Won + Lost); open opportunities are excluded from
  the denominator since their outcome isn't known yet.
- **Data cleaning**: standardized a product-name mismatch ("GTXPro" vs.
  "GTX Pro", 1,480 rows) and a sector typo ("technolgy" → "technology")
  before joining the four source tables.
- All analysis runs on the full population — no sampling.
